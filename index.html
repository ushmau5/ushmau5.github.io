What is TCG Portfolio?

TCG Portfolio is a free Pokémon card collection tracker and price checker for iOS and Android. It shows estimated ungraded and PSA graded values for English and Japanese sets. Add the cards you own and TCG Portfolio adds them up into a total collection value, with eBay sold listings one tap away when you want to check a number yourself.

It is a personal collection and price-tracking tool, not a financial product, and nothing in it is investment advice.

Ungraded conditions and PSA grades

TCG Portfolio prices raw cards by condition: Near Mint (NM), Lightly Played (LP), Moderately Played (MP), Heavily Played (HP) and Damaged (DMG). Graded cards cover PSA 1 (PR) up to PSA 10 (GEM-MT).

Compare what a PSA 10 fetches against a PSA 9 or 8, and against an ungraded copy, before you buy a slab or submit a raw card. The price chart on a card page can show Ungraded or any single PSA grade from 10 down to 1.

Print runs and finishes

TCG Portfolio tracks each printing separately. Print runs are Unlimited, 1st Edition and Shadowless; finishes are Normal, Holofoil, Reverse Holofoil and Anniversary Stamp. A copy is labelled with both, for example "1st Edition · Holofoil", so a 1st Edition holo is never priced as a bulk reverse.

How to add cards

In TCG Portfolio, search for a card by name or browse by set. Then add it as an ungraded copy with its condition, or as a PSA graded slab, along with its print run and finish. Signed-in users can also add a whole set page at a time.

You can search and check prices without an account. Tracking a portfolio needs one, and your collection is saved to it so it follows you to any device.

English and Japanese cards

TCG Portfolio covers English and Japanese sets with the same variant and condition detail. Japanese cards are priced in their own right, not converted from an English equivalent, and the eBay link on a card page includes the card's language. The app itself is in English.

Your collection value over time

The TCG Portfolio portfolio screen shows a total collection value, using the estimated value where price data is available, with a chart for 7D, 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD or 1Y. Sort by most or least valuable, biggest gain or loss, recently added or name. Filter by condition, variant and print run, switch between grid and list view, or hide values. Prices show in USD by default, and you can choose another currency in Settings.

Where do the prices come from?

TCG Portfolio prices are estimates compiled from third-party sources, including eBay and TCGplayer, and are updated regularly. They can be inaccurate, delayed, incomplete or unavailable, and they are not appraisals, offers or guarantees. Each card page has Price, Sold and Buy tabs, with links to eBay sold listings and current eBay listings so you can check the number yourself. How pricing works.

Is TCG Portfolio free?

Yes. TCG Portfolio is free to use and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. It runs on iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later and on Android 7.0 or later. You can delete your account and data in the app or by emailing tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com.
