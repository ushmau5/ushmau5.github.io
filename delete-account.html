TCG Portfolio

Delete your account

Effective 2026-08-29

This page explains how to delete your TCG Portfolio account and the personal data associated with it. TCG Portfolio is operated by Oisin McNally ("we", "us"). You do not need the app installed to request deletion — the email option below works on its own.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open TCG Portfolio and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my TCG Portfolio account".

To protect your account, please send the request from the email address registered to your account. If that is not possible, tell us and we will verify your identity another way before deleting anything. We aim to complete email deletion requests within 30 days, and will confirm once done.

What is deleted

  • Your account data: username, email address, password credentials and email-verification records.
  • Your portfolio data: the cards, quantities, purchase prices and notes you added.

This data is removed from our live systems without undue delay and purged from backups within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

Some data collected before deletion is not erased individually when your account is deleted. It expires automatically under our standard retention periods:

  • Server logs and crash diagnostics — kept for up to 12 months.
  • Session recordings (analytics replays of app screens) — kept for up to 12 months.
  • Analytics event data — kept for up to 12 months.

This means some of this data associated with your past use of the app may persist for up to 12 months after your account is deleted. If you would like it deleted sooner, say so in your email request and we will action it, subject to any legal obligation to retain it. We may also keep limited records where required by law or to resolve disputes or security incidents, and we delete those once no longer needed.

Questions

For full details of how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. For anything else, contact tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com.
