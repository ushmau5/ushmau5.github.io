About TCG Portfolio

Independent since 2021

TCG Portfolio is a free Pokémon card collection tracker and price checker for iOS and Android, built by an independent solo developer in Ireland. It launched on iOS in December 2021 and shows estimated values for ungraded and PSA graded cards from English and Japanese sets.

Who builds TCG Portfolio?

TCG Portfolio is made by one person. It is built and run by an independent solo developer in Ireland, so the questions and feedback you send reach the person who writes the code.

What does TCG Portfolio do?

TCG Portfolio estimates a value for the cards you own, raw or graded, where price data is available, and adds it up into one portfolio value. You can:

  • Search cards by name or browse by set, for English and Japanese sets. Japanese cards are priced in their own right, not converted from an English equivalent.
  • Add a copy as ungraded with its condition or as a PSA graded slab, with its print run and finish. See PSA graded prices for the grades.
  • Follow your total value on charts from 7D to 1Y, then sort and filter your collection by value, gain, condition or print run.
  • Open eBay sold listings for any card in one tap, to check the number yourself.

Tracking a portfolio needs an account, but searching and checking prices do not. See how your collection is valued.

When did TCG Portfolio launch?

TCG Portfolio first launched on the App Store on 28 December 2021. It is now available on both iOS (iOS 16.4 or later) and Android (Android 7.0 or later). The store title is "TCG Portfolio: Card Tracker". On Google Play the app shows 100K+ downloads (as of October 2026), and new sets and pricing data are added frequently.

Are TCG Portfolio prices advice?

No. TCG Portfolio is a personal collection and price-tracking tool, not a financial product, and it does not give investment, financial, tax or legal advice. Trading cards are collectibles, not regulated financial instruments. Every value is an estimate compiled from third-party sources, including eBay and TCGplayer.
