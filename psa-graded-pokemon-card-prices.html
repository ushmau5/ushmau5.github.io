PSA graded Pokémon card prices, raw vs graded

By Oisin McNally, developer of TCG Portfolio · Updated 3 November 2026

PSA graded Pokémon card prices are what a card is worth once PSA has graded it, from PSA 1 to PSA 10. TCG Portfolio, a free Pokémon card price tracker for iOS and Android, shows an estimated value for each PSA grade next to raw (ungraded) prices by condition, so you can compare them on one screen.

How does TCG Portfolio price raw and PSA graded cards?

TCG Portfolio gives every card two condition categories: Ungraded (raw) and PSA. Raw copies are priced by condition: Near Mint (NM), Lightly Played (LP), Moderately Played (MP), Heavily Played (HP) and Damaged (DMG), with Near Mint as the default. Graded copies are priced by PSA grade, with PSA 10 as the default. PSA is the only grading company TCG Portfolio covers today.

How do you compare a PSA 10 with a PSA 9, PSA 8 or raw copy?

Open a card in TCG Portfolio and switch between Ungraded and PSA to see what a PSA 10 is estimated to fetch against a PSA 9 or PSA 8, and against an ungraded copy. Collectors use this gap before they buy a slab or send a raw copy for grading.

The card's price chart can show Ungraded or any single PSA grade from 10 down to 1, over 7D, 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD or 1Y, so you can see how each grade has moved. These figures are information, not advice on whether to buy, sell or grade.

Why do print run, finish and language change the price?

TCG Portfolio prices each printing separately by print run and finish, so a 1st Edition holo is never priced as a bulk reverse holo. See how a price is matched to your exact card.

TCG Portfolio covers English and Japanese sets with the same variant and condition detail. Japanese cards are priced in their own right, not converted from an English equivalent.

Where do TCG Portfolio's graded prices come from?

TCG Portfolio's raw and PSA values are estimates compiled from third-party sources, including eBay and TCGplayer. Read how pricing works and its limits.

To check a number yourself, the card's Sold tab links to eBay sold listings for that card, and the card page also links to current eBay listings. The search includes the card's language.

How do you track PSA slabs in your TCG Portfolio collection?

Search for a card by name or browse by set, then add it to your collection in TCG Portfolio as a PSA graded slab, or as an ungraded copy with its condition, along with its print run and finish. Raw and graded copies sit in one portfolio that shows your total estimated collection value.

  • Sort by Most valuable, Least valuable, Biggest gain, Biggest loss, Recently added or Name.
  • Filter by condition, variant and print run, several at once, to see only your PSA slabs.
  • Values show in USD by default; choose your currency in Settings.

Tracking a portfolio needs an account, but searching and checking prices do not. See how your collection is valued.
