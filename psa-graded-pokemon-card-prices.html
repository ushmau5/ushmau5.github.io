PSA graded Pokémon card prices, ungraded vs graded

PSA 1 to PSA 10, next to the ungraded price, on one screen.

Updated 3 November 2026

PSA graded Pokémon card prices are what a card is worth once PSA has graded it, from PSA 1 to PSA 10. TCG Portfolio, a free Pokémon card price tracker for iOS and Android, shows an estimated value for each PSA grade next to ungraded prices by condition, so you can compare them on one screen.

How does TCG Portfolio price ungraded and PSA graded cards?

TCG Portfolio gives every card two condition categories: Ungraded (ungraded) and PSA. Ungraded copies are priced by condition: Near Mint (NM), Lightly Played (LP), Moderately Played (MP), Heavily Played (HP) and Damaged (DMG), with Near Mint as the default. Graded copies are priced by PSA grade, with PSA 10 as the default. PSA is the only grading company TCG Portfolio covers today.

How do you compare a PSA 10 with a PSA 9, PSA 8 or ungraded copy?

Open a card in TCG Portfolio and switch between Ungraded and PSA to see what a PSA 10 is estimated to fetch against a PSA 9 or PSA 8, and against an ungraded copy. Collectors use this gap before they buy a slab or send an ungraded copy for grading.

On the card page, the Price tab shows a row of tiles: Near Mint first, then each PSA grade with its estimated value. Pick a tile and the market price and chart switch to that condition or grade.

The card's price chart can show Ungraded or any single PSA grade from 10 down to 1, over 7D, 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD or 1Y, so you can see how each grade has moved. These figures are information, not advice on whether to buy, sell or grade.

Why do print run and finish change a graded price?

TCG Portfolio prices each printing separately by print run and finish, so a 1st Edition holo is never priced as a bulk reverse holo. See how a price is matched to your exact card.

Print runs are Unlimited, 1st Edition and Shadowless. Finishes are Normal, Holofoil, Reverse Holofoil and Anniversary Stamp. A printing is labelled with both, such as "1st Edition · Holofoil", and a PSA 10 of one printing is a different line from a PSA 10 of another.

Are Japanese cards priced by PSA grade too?

Yes. TCG Portfolio covers English and Japanese sets with the same variant and condition detail, so a Japanese card has the same Ungraded and PSA 1 to PSA 10 options as an English one. Japanese cards are priced in their own right, not converted from an English equivalent, and the eBay search from a Japanese card includes its language. The app itself is in English. Where do TCG Portfolio's graded prices come from?

TCG Portfolio's ungraded and PSA values are estimates compiled from third-party sources, including eBay and TCGplayer. Read how pricing works and its limits.

To check a number yourself, the card's Sold tab links to eBay sold listings for that card, and the card page also links to current eBay listings. The sold listings are free for everyone.

How current are graded price estimates?

Prices in TCG Portfolio are updated regularly, and new sets and pricing data are added often. Each figure is still an estimate: it may be inaccurate, delayed, incomplete or unavailable, and it is not an appraisal, an offer or a guarantee of what a slab will sell for. Before you rely on a PSA value, compare it with recent eBay sold listings for the same card, grade and printing.

Is the app connected to PSA?

No. TCG Portfolio is an independent personal collection and price-tracking tool. PSA is a trademark of its owner and the name is used only to identify the grade on a card. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by PSA or any grading company.

How do you track PSA slabs in your TCG Portfolio collection?

Search for a card by name or browse by set, then add it to your collection in TCG Portfolio as a PSA graded slab, or as an ungraded copy with its condition, along with its print run and finish. Ungraded and graded copies sit in one portfolio that shows your total estimated collection value.

  • Sort by Most valuable, Least valuable, Biggest gain, Biggest loss, Recently added or Name.
  • Filter by condition, variant and print run, several at once, to see only your PSA slabs.
  • Values show in USD by default; choose your currency in Settings.

The portfolio chart shows how that total has moved over 7D, 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD or 1Y, and a setting can hide values on screen. To add many cards at once, long-press to select a whole set page on a set screen.

Tracking a portfolio needs an account, but searching and checking prices do not. The collection is saved to your account and follows you to any device. See how your collection is valued.
