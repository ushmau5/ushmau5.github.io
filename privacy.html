Effective 2026-10-29 · Version 1.1

Who we are. TCG Portfolio ("the App") is operated by Oisin McNally, a sole trader established in Ireland ("we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact / postal address: Oisin McNally, Earlspark, Loughrea, H62 WK79, Co. Galway, Ireland. Email: tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com

Scope. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the App on iOS or Android, why, on what legal basis, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and your rights. The App is available worldwide; most of our users are in the US, UK, Canada and the EU/EEA.

A quick, honest summary. You need an account to track your portfolio, so we process your email address and login data. The core App is free and supported by advertising, so advertising partners may process device and usage data to show you ads. If we offer an optional paid subscription and you buy it, we also process your purchase status through our subscription provider (see section 1). We use your portfolio and how you use the App to personalise what it shows you, for example listings from marketplaces such as eBay and TCGplayer for cards missing from your collection. Some links to these marketplaces are affiliate links that may earn us a commission. We do not send marketing emails or newsletters, but we may email a few users one to one to ask for feedback. We do not sell your personal data for money. However, showing personalised ads can count as a "sale", "sharing" of personal information, or "targeted advertising" under some US state privacy laws — you can opt out (see section 7). We do not run any social, sharing, messaging or user-content features.

2. Advertising, tracking and your choices

The core App is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may fill some ad requests through its mediation partner Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), whose software is built into the App. For ad personalisation, Google and Unity generally act as independent controllers of the data they collect through their SDKs, not merely as our processors.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before serving personalised ads, we ask for your consent through a Google-certified Consent Management Platform using the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. Your choices also apply to our ad partners, including Unity Ads. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalised ads. You can change your choices at any time in the App's privacy/consent settings.
  • Analytics and session recordings: product analytics (PostHog) is enabled by default. It records how you use the App together with your device details (device type, operating system and version, app version, screen dimensions, timezone), your approximate city-level location estimated from your IP address, and, where you are signed in, your email address and account identifier. We also use PostHog session replay, which records your interactions with the App's screens — this can include your portfolio contents and estimated values as they appear on screen. Analytics and recordings are used to understand and improve the App. Analytics (not session recordings) is also used to personalise suggestions in the App and to choose users to invite to give feedback. Neither is used for advertising, and both are kept for up to 12 months. You can opt out at any time in the App's settings, which stops further analytics collection and session recording from your device.
  • Apple devices (iOS): where we or our ad partners would "track" you across other companies' apps and websites, iOS shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline (or we do not request tracking), you will receive non-personalised ads.
  • US states: you can opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising (see section 7), and we honour the Global Privacy Control signal where required.

Learn more / manage choices: AdMob & Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads); Unity Ads (https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

Emails. We do not send marketing emails or newsletters. We send emails you need for your account (such as sign-in codes and email verification) and important notices about the App, our Terms or this policy. We may also email a small number of users one to one to ask for feedback, invite them to an interview, or invite them to test a new feature before release (see "Research emails" in section 1). Taking part is optional. These emails never advertise anything, and you can reply "stop" to any of them. Interviews and testing are for adults (18 and over) only, and we record an interview only if you agree at the start.

Suggestions and affiliate links. We use your portfolio and how you use the App to personalise what you see, for example listings from marketplaces such as eBay and TCGplayer for cards missing from your collection. These suggestions are not paid ads: no seller pays us to show them. Many links to these marketplaces are affiliate links: if you buy after opening one, we may earn a commission. To find listings, the marketplace receives only a search for the card (such as its name, number and set). A link you open contains that search and our affiliate tracking code, but not your name, email address, account identifier or portfolio. Once you are on their site, the marketplace may use cookies and similar technologies to record that you came from TCG Portfolio, and their own privacy policies apply. Suggestions are made automatically, but they only change which cards and listings you see; we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. You can object to personalised suggestions at any time (see section 6).

Push notifications. We send push notifications only if you allow them when your device asks. We use them for things like price alerts on cards you track and notices about new or changed features, not for advertising. To stop them, turn off notifications for TCG Portfolio in your device settings. This withdraws your consent and does not affect the rest of the App.

5. Data retention

We keep account and portfolio data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (from within the App, via our account deletion page, or by emailing us at tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com) we delete your account and portfolio data from our live systems without undue delay and purge them from backups within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records to comply with law or to resolve disputes or security incidents, which we delete once no longer needed. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Residual data collected before deletion — server logs, crash diagnostics, session recordings and event-level analytics — is not individually erased when your account is deleted; it expires automatically under its retention period, which in each case is up to 12 months. This means some of this data may persist for up to 12 months after account deletion. If you would like it deleted sooner, email tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com and we will action your request, subject to any legal obligation to retain it.

6. Your GDPR/UK rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests (for analytics, the quickest way is the opt-out toggle in the App's settings), and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). To exercise these rights, email tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission — 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, Ireland; info@dataprotection.ie; +353 1 765 0100; www.dataprotection.ie — or with your local supervisory authority. UK users may complain to the UK ICO (ico.org.uk).

Your right to object. You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, including analytics, personalised suggestions and research emails. For analytics, use the opt-out in the App's settings. To stop personalised suggestions or research emails, email tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com, or reply "stop" to any research email. Because suggestions can include affiliate links to items you could buy, if you object to them we will stop, and you do not need to give a reason.

Depending on your state (e.g., California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky, Rhode Island), you may have the right to access, delete, correct and port your data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising. To opt out, use the in-app "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information / Opt out of targeted advertising" control, decline ad-personalisation consent, or email tcgportfoliodev@gmail.com. We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where required. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
